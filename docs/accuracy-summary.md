# How to interpret this reconstruction

This is a recognizable exterior study assembled from dated public evidence. It
is not a survey, a certified account of every current feature, or a claim of
photorealism. The long [visual review record](visual-accuracy.md) preserves the
iterations, rejected treatments and individual corrections.

| Part | Evidence used | Remaining limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Site and ownership | Confirmed headquarters address and 7.00-acre tax parcel; Spring 2026 county aerial | Adjacent parking, buildings and landscape are context, not all Protolabs property. Parcel mapping is not a legal survey. |
| Roofs and terrain | 2022 lidar constrains roof planes, elevations and the near terrain; a coarser USGS DEM supplies distant ground | Different dates, coordinate datums and sampling densities limit exact alignment. Small curbs, drainage and ground transitions are interpreted. |
| Entrance and facade | Published architectural and company photographs support the distinctive roof, truss, glazing divisions, canopy and signs | Hidden trim, some door arrangements, exact profiles, roof equipment and rear/service details are incomplete or inferred. Substitute lettering is not exact corporate typography. |
| Parking and landscaping | Aerial traces establish pavement, islands, rows and visible planting; lidar supports selected tree envelopes | Markings and occupancy can change. Aerial bay traces are not certified capacity or accessibility specifications. Species, individual crown shape, fine planting and seasonal condition are not verified. |
| Pond and distant woods | A dated lidar elevation supports a flat water interpretation; traced belts and conservative 2025 NLCD forest interiors guide distant cover | Shoreline is coarse and does not establish current water level or bathymetry. Wetland vegetation and distant settlement detail are incomplete; the open background differs from the reference photograph. |
| Materials and lighting | Licensed CC0 surface scans and a low-sun environment; original vehicle and vegetation assets | These are visual proxies, not scans of the building. Weathering, glass reflections, lawn variation, cars and repeated tree forms still look synthetic. The environment is not a registered local sky photograph. |
| Interior and interactive view | Exterior geometry and simplified glass backing; a lightweight browser export | No accurate interior is asserted. The browser uses mean-color daylight materials and simpler distant crowns, so it does not reproduce Blender's textures, reflections or lighting. Performance depends on hardware. |

The strongest evidence is the building's overall geometry and its relationship
to the measured site. The weakest areas are current fine detail, the rear and
service elevations, individual vegetation and the distant surroundings. Camera
fit residuals describe selected image points; they do not measure whole-image
resemblance. A completed export or a passing geometry check cannot establish
visual fidelity.
