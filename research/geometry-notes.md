## Shape and layout to preserve

- Long horizontal low-rise headquarters, with contrasting darker left block and lighter right wing. Flat roofs on wings; central silver standing-seam roof is an expressive single sloped plane, not a symmetric gable.
- In front view the entrance roof pitches down left-to-right. Its high thin tip projects beyond the enclosure. Open white steel trusses and braces below the projecting edge are a defining landmark.
- Main support is a dark cylindrical column forward of the wall. White diagonal members fan from its head toward the sloped leading edge and vestibule-side support. Shallow triangular web truss follows that edge. A glazed triangular clerestory fills the back portion.
- Rectangular glass entry vestibule is under the low half of the roof, with double glass doors and transom. White fascia above has 5540; gray/blue mullions form regular panes. Smaller entry lies left under a broad flat white logo fascia.
- White right-wing fascia sits over a long dark blue/black glazing ribbon with narrow vertical mullions and a horizontal transom. Glass wraps the oblique corner immediately right of entry. Several broad white piers interrupt the glazing farther right; final white end panel has a service door.
- Left block has a higher flat parapet, alternating broad subtle vertical brown/red panel bands and a regular row of narrow portrait windows. Recessed/stepped left extremity is partly hidden by trees.
- Roof equipment: one large rectangular white screen over left block; multiple dark mechanical units behind the center roof; white rectangular screen near center/rear; another broad white screen on the right flat roof, plus exposed compact duct/fan units.
- Site: rock beds at facade, shrubs by glazing, broad asphalt parking and drive, accessible stalls to entry right, central flagpole/rock island with red hydrant, separate dark monument sign near driveway; mature deciduous trees to left/back and open vegetation beyond right/back.

## Proportions and scale (confidence explicitly bounded)

- Official 2025 procurement guide fixes reported total/office area at 95,800 ft² (8,900.11 m²). It does not say footprint or number of storeys; use actual footprint source separately.
- Frontage composition in the wide aerial: darker left block roughly 30% of projected building width, center entry/roof about 25%, long right wing about 45%. These are perspective image ratios, not plan measurements.
- Right glazed ribbon appears about 1.5–2 times the height of the white fascia above it. It is subdivided into a short upper band, tall middle panes, and short lower band at the corner by the entrance.
- Left narrow windows appear roughly 2.2–2.8 times taller than wide. Their center spacing is roughly 2–2.5 window widths. Exact count is partly obscured and should follow best image.
- The entry vestibule is roughly 2.5–3 door leaves wide across the front and approximately 1.5 door heights tall including transom/fascia. One leaf of the double door provides a conventional human-scale anchor, not a measured dimension.
- Visible projecting roof edge has front elevation slope roughly 25–35 degrees relative to level in near-frontal ground photo. Camera perspective affects this; high roof tip is about twice adjacent right-wall height.
- Do not derive absolute site dimensions from the oblique photos. Model heights should remain labelled inferred until a survey or reliable elevation is found.

## Exclusions / unresolved

- Numerous web articles mislabel the white two-storey building marked 8500 as headquarters. That is 8500 Wyoming Avenue N, Brooklyn Park manufacturing; not this building.
- HCM cached portfolio has internally conflicting Maple Plain/Plymouth and 87,510/170,000 ft² text. Its numbers have not been used.
- No surveyed elevation, as-built plans or reliable original architect identification was recovered. Rear geometry and unseen roof equipment are not resolved by these photographs.
- File year 2024 is image filename metadata, not independently verified capture date. Ground images published 2018/2021/2024 are history checks, not proof of current condition.
