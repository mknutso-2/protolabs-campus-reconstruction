How to interpret this reconstruction

An independent exterior study assembled from dated public evidence. This is not an official Protolabs website, a survey, a certified account of every current feature, or a claim of photorealism.

PartEvidence usedRemaining limitation

Materials and lightingLicensed CC0 surface scans and a low-sun environment; original vehicle and vegetation assetsThese are visual proxies, not scans of the building. Weathering, glass reflections, lawn variation, cars and repeated tree forms still look synthetic. The environment is not a registered local sky photograph.
Interior and interactive viewExterior geometry and simplified glass backing; a lightweight browser exportNo accurate interior is asserted. The browser uses mean-color daylight materials and simpler distant crowns, so it does not reproduce Blender's textures, reflections or lighting. Thin paint and roof features can alias at shallow angles, even with a sharper resting image. Performance depends on hardware.

The strongest evidence is the building's overall geometry and its relationship to the measured site. The weakest areas are current fine detail, the rear and service elevations, individual vegetation and the distant surroundings. Camera fit residuals describe selected image points; they do not measure whole-image resemblance. A completed export or a passing geometry check cannot establish visual fidelity.
